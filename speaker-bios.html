Amedeo Chiribiri

Professor of Cardiovascular Imaging · King’s College London

Professor Amedeo Chiribiri received his medical degree from the University of Turin in 2001, completed cardiology training in 2006, and earned a PhD in Experimental Medicine and Human Physiology in 2011. During his cardiology training, he developed an interest in non-invasive cardiovascular imaging, including echocardiography, cardiac computed tomography, and magnetic resonance imaging.

He leads the Cardiac MRI Service at Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust and is Professor of Cardiovascular Imaging at King’s College London. He has authored or co-authored more than 300 scientific papers and several book chapters. His research focuses on high-resolution, non-invasive quantification of myocardial perfusion and assessment of coronary pathophysiology.

Victoria Delgado

Head of Cardiovascular Imaging · Germans Trias i Pujol

Victoria Delgado is Head of the Cardiovascular Imaging section of the Heart Institute (iCor) at Germans Trias i Pujol University Hospital, with a focus on multimodality imaging. She earned her medical degree at the University of Barcelona, specialized in cardiology at Hospital Clínic Barcelona, and completed her PhD with cum laude at Leiden University. She worked as a senior cardiology consultant at Leiden University Medical Center for 13 years.

She is President of the European Association of Cardiovascular Imaging. She served as a councillor on the European Society of Cardiology board from 2020 to 2022 and has contributed to Society committees, including the EuroObservational Research Programme and clinical practice guidelines. She has been Senior Editor of Circulation and serves on the boards of international peer-reviewed journals. She is also a Scientific Advisory Board member of the European Cardiovascular Research Institute and President of the Scientific Commission of the Fund for Scientific Research (F.R.S.–FNRS).

Inés García-Lunar

Cardiologist and Group Leader · CNIC

Dr. Inés García-Lunar is a cardiologist specializing in non-invasive cardiovascular imaging and a group leader at the National Center for Cardiovascular Research (CNIC) in Madrid. She leads the Cardiovascular Prevention Through Non-Invasive Imaging group and, with Dr. Valentín Fuster, serves as clinical leader of the scientific programme on Novel Mechanisms of Atherosclerosis.

After completing her medical and cardiology training in Spain, she specialized in advanced cardiovascular imaging at Mount Sinai Hospital in New York. She has more than 15 years of experience in clinical cardiology, cardiovascular imaging, and translational research. She heads the imaging committees of the PESA and REACT studies. Her research uses multimodality imaging to detect and track early subclinical atherosclerosis and translate findings into precision cardiovascular prevention.

José Tomás Ortiz-Pérez

Consultant Cardiologist · Hospital Clínic Barcelona

José Tomás Ortiz-Pérez, MD, PhD, is a consultant cardiologist at the Cardiovascular Institute of Hospital Clínic Barcelona and Associate Professor of Medicine at the University of Barcelona. Since 2008, he has worked in the hospital’s Acute Cardiovascular Care Unit and Cardiac Imaging Unit. He earned his medical degree at the University of Navarra, completed cardiology residency and an interventional cardiology fellowship at Hospital Clínic, then undertook advanced cardiovascular magnetic resonance and cardiac computed tomography training at Northwestern University in Chicago.

He received his PhD with highest honors from the University of Barcelona for research on myocardial injury after acute myocardial infarction using cardiovascular magnetic resonance. He is Co-Director of the University of Barcelona’s Master’s Program in Acute and Perioperative Cardiac Intensive Care and a member of Hospital Clínic’s Amyloidosis and Multiple Myeloma Unit. His research focuses on post-infarction ventricular remodeling, multimodality cardiac imaging, and cardiac amyloidosis. He has authored more than 110 peer-reviewed publications.

Andreu Climent

CEO and Co-founder · Corify Care

Andreu M. Climent, PhD, is an electronic engineer (PhD, Universitat Politècnica de València, 2011) and the visionary behind Corify Care, a medtech company transforming cardiac arrhythmia treatment.

Key highlights

  • Clinical Impact: Successfully brought to market a non-invasive 4D real-time global cardiac mapping system, now utilized in top-tier global hospitals such as the Mayo Clinic (USA) and Charité (Berlin).
  • Regulatory Success: Secured both CE Mark and FDA approval for Corify Care’s core technology.
  • Research & Leadership: Former head of translational cardiac electrophysiology at Hospital Gregorio Marañón and deputy director of CIBERCV.
  • Scientific Contribution: Authored 70+ peer-reviewed papers, holds multiple patents, and leads major European-funded projects.
  • Awards: Named European Innovator of the Year by the European Institute of Innovation and Technology.

Pedro Correia

Assistant Researcher · University of Aveiro

Pedro Correia is an Assistant Researcher at the University of Aveiro (UA), Portugal, within the Radiation Detection and Medical Imaging (DRIM) group at i3N – Instituto de Nanoestruturas, Nanomodelação e Nanofabricação (Aveiro).

During his PhD in Physics Engineering, he developed positron emission tomography (PET) systems. His research focuses on radiation detector instrumentation, preclinical and clinical nuclear imaging systems, Monte Carlo simulation, and AI-driven image reconstruction. He is co-inventor of 3 international patents and has several collaborations with academia and industry. He has also carried out short-term stays as a visiting researcher at institutions such as CERN, the Department of Radiology at the University of California San Francisco (UCSF, USA), and the Paul Scherrer Institute (Switzerland). Through the “la Caixa” Foundation’s CaixaImpulse 2025 programme, he is working on the project “Theragamma”, which aims to develop advanced gamma radiation detectors for precision medicine and theranostics.

Lino Ferreira

Investigator Coordinator · Faculty of Medicine, University of Coimbra

Lino Ferreira is a Principal Investigator at the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Coimbra, where he founded and directs the Advanced Therapies Laboratory. He is the founder and director of the Colab4Ageing Collaborative Laboratory and the coordinator of the “Institute for Integrated Social and Biological Assessment”, a Teaming project funded by the European Community and the Portuguese Government, as well as the deputy director of CIBB, an Associate Laboratory of the University of Coimbra in the field of biomedicine.

A primary focus of his laboratory is the development of technology platforms to attenuate the effects of ageing. The laboratory aims to investigate age-related diseases, particularly those linked to the cardiovascular system, and to develop pharmacological interventions to mitigate the effects of aging. He has authored over 180 scientific publications, filed more than 25 international patents, secured over 18 million euros in competitive funding, and trained more than 30 PhD students and 20 postdoctoral researchers.

José Javier Fuster

Associate Professor and Group Leader · CNIC

José Javier Fuster, PhD, is an Associate Professor and Group Leader of the Hematovascular Pathophysiology Laboratory at the Spanish National Center for Cardiovascular Research (CNIC) in Madrid, where he also serves as Coordinator of the Scientific Program on Novel Mechanisms of Atherosclerosis. Prior to joining CNIC, he conducted postdoctoral research at Boston University and held faculty appointments at Boston University and the University of Virginia.

His research focuses on the interplay between the hematopoietic and cardiovascular systems in cardiovascular disease, with particular emphasis on the pathophysiology of atherosclerosis. Dr. Fuster leads an interdisciplinary research program that combines the analysis of biological samples from deeply phenotyped human cohorts with mechanistic studies in advanced mouse models. He also holds major scientific roles in two flagship CNIC-led population-imaging studies, the PESA and REACT studies, with leadership roles in systems biology and genetics.

He is internationally recognized for his pioneering work on age-related clonal hematopoiesis and its contribution to cardiovascular disease, helping establish this field as a major area of investigation in cardiovascular medicine. His work has been published in leading scientific journals, including Nature, Science, Nature Medicine, European Heart Journal, Journal of the American College of Cardiology, Cell Metabolism and Nature Cardiovascular Research, among others. He has received several distinctions, including Young Investigator Awards from the European Atherosclerosis Society and the European Society for Clinical Investigation and the European Society of Cardiology Outstanding Achievement Award.

Ana García-Álvarez

Head of Cardiology · Hospital Clínic Barcelona · University of Barcelona

Dr. Ana García-Álvarez (MD, PhD) is Head of the Cardiology Department at Hospital Clínic de Barcelona, where she also leads the Familial Cardiomyopathy Unit, and Associate Professor and Vice-Dean of the Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences at the University of Barcelona. She completed her Cardiology residency at Hospital Clínic and obtained her PhD cum laude at the University of Barcelona. She further specialized in advanced cardiovascular imaging at Mount Sinai Hospital in New York and in biostatistics and epidemiology at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health.

She is group leader at IDIBAPS and at the National Center for Cardiovascular Research (CNIC), where her translational research focuses on pulmonary hypertension, right ventricular dysfunction and inherited cardiac diseases, from experimental models to multicenter clinical trials. She has published over 120 articles in first-quartile journals and has been principal investigator of multiple competitive research projects. She is a nucleus member of the ESC Working Group on Pulmonary Circulation and Right Ventricular Function and an evaluator for international funding agencies.

Annalaura Mastrangelo

Researcher · CNIC, Madrid

Annalaura Mastrangelo, PharmD, PhD, is a researcher at the Spanish National Center for Cardiovascular Research (CNIC) in Madrid, Spain. Her work focuses on understanding how microbial metabolism influences cardiovascular and cardiometabolic diseases through the integration of metabolomics, multi-omics technologies, computational analyses, human cohort studies, and animal models.

Her research has led to the identification of microbiota-derived metabolites involved in the development of atherosclerosis and other chronic diseases, opening new opportunities for early diagnosis and therapeutic intervention. She is particularly interested in translating mechanistic discoveries into clinically relevant biomarkers and therapeutic strategies, while also promoting rigorous and reproducible research practices and the development of open computational tools for biomedical data analysis.

Andreia Pereira

PhD Researcher · i3S, Porto

Andreia Pereira is a PhD researcher at i3S – Instituto de Investigação e Inovação em Saúde, Porto, where she leads multidisciplinary projects at the intersection of biomaterials, biointerfaces and self-powered sensing. Her research focuses on developing biomaterials for cardiovascular applications, including vascular grafts, stents and cardiac patches, using non-fouling surface strategies and tissue engineering approaches.

A key focus is the development of smart vascular grafts that combine improved blood compatibility with autonomous monitoring for the early detection of complications. Her goal is to translate interface science into clinically relevant solutions for safer and more personalized cardiovascular care.
